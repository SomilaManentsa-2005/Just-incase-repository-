Our Services

Founded in 2023 in Nelson Mandela Bay, Kasi-Tech Solutions began as a small freelance IT collective providing local hardware support and basic computer network setups. Over the past three years, it has expanded into a full-service technology consulting firm aiding local businesses with systems analysis, network infrastructure, and custom web applications.

Services We Provide

Web Application

Designing and building tailored, responsive multi-page websites and online web tools to drive customer growth through direct online consultation/enquiry forms, and allows potential clients to browse services anytime.

Network Infrastructure

Designing and setting up reliable office networks, Wi-Fi routers, firewalls, and cabling solutions to keep your business connected, secure, and running smoothly.

Database & System Analysis

Small business data design, customer order management, and system workflow optimization to maintain clean, reliable, and secure data operations.
