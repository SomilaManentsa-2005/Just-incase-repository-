About Our Enterprise

Our History

Founded in 2023 in Nelson Mandela Bay, Kasi-Tech Solutions began as a small freelance IT collective providing local hardware support and basic computer network setups. Over the past three years, it has expanded into a full-service technology consulting firm aiding local businesses with systems analysis, network infrastructure, and custom web applications.

Mission Statement

To empower local small-to-medium enterprises by delivering robust, scalable, and secure digital infrastructure and modern web solutions tailored to operational needs.

Vision Statement

To become the premier technology and web development provider in the Eastern Cape, driving local business growth through modern software tools and digital transformation.
